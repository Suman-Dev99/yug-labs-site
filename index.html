Frequently Asked Questions

Curious about Yug Labs?
We've got you covered.

What exactly is the Lab? +

Your own workspace for building websites, with AI help. It's not open yet — login and online payment are still being built, so nobody can create an account today. When it opens: one project = one website, editing by hand is free, and you spend AI credits when you want the AI to draft something. More on the Lab page.

How do I order and pay? +

Pick a plan, tap Order, fill the form, send it on WhatsApp. We confirm scope and total in the chat before anything is paid. There's no online checkout yet — PayPal and UPI (via Razorpay / Cashfree) come later.

What is a credit, in plain words? +

A credit is a small unit of AI work. Doing things yourself — typing, editing, moving blocks, changing colours — is free, always. Asking the AI to draft a page or write a section costs credits, usually 10–15 for a page draft, and the cost shows before it runs. Credits refresh every month.

Lab plan or done-for-you — what is the difference? +

Lab plan: you build, in your own workspace, for a monthly price. Done-for-you: we build and hand over a finished site, for one payment. They're separate — a Lab plan never includes a site built by our team, and a done-for-you package never includes Lab credits.

Why are prices in US dollars? +

So everyone, wherever they are, sees the same price. Orders go through WhatsApp for now — no online checkout yet (see Payment methods on the Pricing page).

Can I get a refund? +

Yes, in the cases in our Refund Policy. Short version: cancel before work starts and you get a full refund. Lab plans can be cancelled any time and stop at the end of the paid month.
