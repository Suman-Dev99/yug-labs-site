Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

What we collect

Your name and email for your account; transaction details processed through Razorpay (your card details never reach us); your projects and builds inside the Lab; and the technical logs needed to run the service.

Why we collect it

For three things only: running your account, verifying payment to open Lab access, and keeping your projects saved. Your data is never sold.

Connectors

When you connect an account such as GitHub or Vercel, we keep only the access token that publishing requires. Disconnecting removes the token.

Your rights (DPDP Act)

  • See and export your data
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete your account and data
  • Raise a grievance: admin@yuglab.online

Changes

If this policy changes, this page updates and the date above changes with it. Major changes are also announced by email.
