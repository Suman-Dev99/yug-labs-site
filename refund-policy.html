Refund Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

First rule — a failed build means credits back

If the Lab cannot produce a complete package for a build, every credit that build spent returns to your balance in full. A complete package, or a complete credit refund — nothing in between.

Subscription refunds

  • Within 7 days of payment, if Lab access was never used — a full refund
  • An accidental double payment — the extra payment returned in full
  • Credits already spent on completed builds are not refunded in cash

How to claim

Write to admin@yuglab.online with your payment ID. Approved refunds reach the original payment method within 5–7 working days, through Razorpay.

Cancellation

Cancel anytime — access continues to the end of the current cycle and no further charge is made.
